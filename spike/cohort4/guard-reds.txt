== two misfires during this falsification, kept because they are the point ==
- The first 4a mutant targeted a variable (himalaya_commit=) that no
  longer exists in the rewritten script; sed matched nothing, the mutant
  was byte-identical, and the run came back green. A green falsification
  run is a broken falsification until proven otherwise.
- The first 4a rc was read through a pipe (sh mutant.sh | tail -1; echo
  $?) and reported 0 while the FAIL line was printing: tail's rc, the C1
  register row, stepped in again and caught by re-reading the raw rc.
