  Two things this does NOT say.

     The frozen operation carries no -times, and the control variant
     above shows its mtime differing between runs. The elimination
     holds in the -times variants, and the point of measuring them is
     narrow: even with the mtime pinned, the archive still differs, so
     amending the frozen argv would not buy determinism. It is not a
     claim that the shipped run has no mtime term.

     which the freeze named as the un-coverable residue, is measured
     above and does not move, so the freeze's forecast was wrong about
     which term survives. What does move is unattributed, and stays
     recorded that way.

  on one pre-state do not agree, so condition 5 fails and unison is a
  named wall of the nondeterministic-writer class, recorded at probe
