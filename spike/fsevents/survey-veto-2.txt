   Containment passes through coalescing and reordering by construction,

   from the shim. Judging FSEvents against the shim in an experiment

   operation is either a false alarm or a real miss, and that comparison
   cannot say which.

   weaker than oracle_verified reopens the contract (#201, #202, #156).

== what this survey does not answer

   the rate: containment was measured on one probe in an idle directory,
